## 🇬🇧 English Clinical Scenarios

### 1. Acute Upper Respiratory Infection (URI) & Pharyngitis
> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"Patient is a 32-year-old presenting with a high-grade fever, sore throat, and productive cough for the past 3 days. On physical examination, body temperature is 101.4 degrees Fahrenheit, pulse is 84 beats per minute, blood pressure is 120 over 80 millimeters of mercury, and oxygen saturation is 98 percent on room air. Oropharynx inspection reveals bilateral tonsillar erythema without purulent exudates. Chest auscultation is clear bilaterally. Prescribing Paracetamol 650 milligrams three times daily after food for 3 days and Cetirizine 10 milligrams at bedtime. Advised warm saline gargles, steam inhalation, and oral hydration."*

---

### 2. Cardiology & Chest Tightness Evaluation
> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"Patient presents with retrosternal chest discomfort and occasional palpitations for 2 days, aggravated after climbing stairs. Blood pressure is elevated at 142 over 88 millimeters of mercury, regular heart rate of 92 beats per minute, respiratory rate 18, oxygen saturation 99 percent. Cardiovascular examination shows regular S1 and S2 sounds with no murmurs or gallops. 12-lead electrocardiogram demonstrates normal sinus rhythm with no acute ischemic ST-segment elevation. Ordering serial cardiac Troponin I and a complete fasting lipid profile. Prescribing Metoprolol Tartrate 25 milligrams twice daily with meals and sublingual Nitroglycerin as needed."*

---

### 3. Gastroenterology — GERD & Acid Reflux
> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"42-year-old patient reports severe burning retrosternal sensation, acid regurgitation, and postprandial bloating for 3 weeks, exacerbated when lying down after heavy meals. Blood pressure is 124 over 78, heart rate 72 bpm, weight 74 kilograms. Abdominal exam is soft and non-distended, with mild epigastric tenderness upon deep palpation. No organomegaly, normal active bowel sounds. Diagnosis of gastroesophageal reflux disease. Prescribing Pantoprazole 40 milligrams once daily 30 minutes before breakfast for 4 weeks and Magaldrate antacid suspension 10 milliliters after meals. Advised dietary modifications: avoid spicy meals, late-night snacking, and elevate head of bed."*

---

### 4. Orthopedics — Knee Joint Osteoarthritis
> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"62-year-old patient presents with bilateral knee pain, predominantly on the right medial joint line for 4 months. Reports morning stiffness lasting 20 minutes and crepitus upon standing. Blood pressure 130 over 82 mmHg, BMI 29.1. Right knee physical exam reveals mild joint effusion, medial joint line tenderness, range of motion 0 to 115 degrees. X-ray bilateral knees demonstrates medial joint space narrowing and subchondral sclerosis. Prescribing Aceclofenac 100 milligrams plus Paracetamol 325 milligrams twice daily with meals for 7 days, Glucosamine sulfate 1500 milligrams daily, and referred for quadriceps strengthening physical therapy."*

---

### 5. Dermatology — Allergic Contact Dermatitis
> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"Patient presents with an intensely pruritic erythematous rash across both forearms and neck for 5 days following the use of a new cosmetic lotion. Vitals: temperature 98.4°F, pulse 76 beats per minute. Skin inspection reveals well-demarcated erythematous plaques with superficial excoriations and mild scaling. Prescribing Hydrocortisone 1 percent topical cream twice daily for 7 days, Levocetirizine 5 milligrams at bedtime for 5 days, and advised gentle soap substitutes and fragrance-free emollient moisturizers."*

---

### 6. Chronic Care — Type 2 Diabetes & Hypertension Follow-Up
> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"58-year-old established patient here for routine diabetes and hypertension management. Blood pressure is 148 over 92, heart rate 76, BMI 28.4. Patient reports occasional morning headaches and mild fatigue for 2 weeks. Current medication is Metformin 500 milligrams twice daily. Adding Lisinopril 10 milligrams daily for blood pressure control and ordered fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c, and serum creatinine tests."*

## 🌍 Multilingual Talking Scripts

> **Speak into Microphone:**  
> *"मरीज को 3 दिनों से तेज बुखार, गले में खराश और सूखी खांसी की शिकायत है। शरीर का तापमान 101.4 डिग्री फारेनहाइट मापा गया है। बीपी 120/80 mmHg और पल्स 84 प्रति मिनट है। गले में हल्की लालिमा है। मरीज को पेरासिटामोल 650 मिलीग्राम दिन में तीन बार 3 दिन के लिए और सेट्रिज़िन 10 मिलीग्राम रात को लेने की सलाह दी गई है। साथ ही गर्म पानी की भाप और आराम करने को कहा गया है।"*

Al examen físico se observa eritema faríngeo sin exudados. Se prescribe Paracetamol 650 miligramos cada 8 horas por 3 días y Cetirizina 10 miligramos por la noche.
